# AstroGuide Knowledge Framework

AstroGuide is designed to support multiple traditional and interpretive systems without presenting them as scientifically proven facts.

## Supported interpretation layers
1. Vedic Jyotish — birth chart factors, signs, houses, nakshatra and planetary relationships when a calculation engine is connected.
2. Lal Kitab — rule-based remedial and interpretive traditions, presented with context and cautions.
3. Samudrika Shastra — optional user-provided observations or images, with privacy consent; interpretations must be framed as traditional beliefs rather than factual personality or future claims.
4. Bhrigu traditions — structured interpretive themes attributed to Bhrigu-style astrology; no claim that a specific person's ancient manuscript has been located unless independently verified.
5. User-selected practical context — career, relationships, personal goals and questions.

## Trust rules
- Show which interpretation system contributed to an answer.
- Separate calculated chart data from interpretive commentary.
- Do not claim 100% certainty or guaranteed future events.
- Do not use fear, curses, dosha threats or payment pressure.
- Never claim a copyrighted/private text has been fully reproduced or embedded without rights.
- Encourage professional help for medical, legal and financial decisions.

## Future architecture
Calculation engine -> normalized chart data -> tradition-specific rule modules -> evidence/explanation layer -> conversational AI.
